Bonnie Mehring

Bonnie is a Senior Project Manager at the Free Software Foundation Europe. There she is currently hosting the Software Freedom Podcast, and organising the I Love Free Software Day, the Youth Hacking 4 Freedom programming competition and is helping to support the German Team as a deputy coordinator.

Bonnie and Free Software

Bonnie has had an interest in Free Software for over 10 years. She first learned about Free Software at the local hackerspace, where she got also introduced to the Chaos Communication Congress and ever since then she loves to go there year after year! Bonnie studied political science and economical geography, in her studies she always tried to connect them with Free Software and has written her master thesis about the digital dependency of public bodies on a local level in Germany.

Through Free Software, Bonnie has not only acquired extensive knowledge about computers and technology but has also forged connections with others. She thoroughly enjoys participating in various Free Software conferences and events, where she relishes the opportunity to connect with individuals from the Free Software community. It is of utmost importance to Bonnie that inclusive and welcoming Free Software communities are established, allowing everyone to engage and learn.

Public speaking

Bonnie loves to give talks about the Podcast, I Love Free Software Day, Youth Hacking 4 Freedom, and "Public Money? Public Code!".
