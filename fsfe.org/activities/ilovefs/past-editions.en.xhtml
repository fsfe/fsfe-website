Edition 2026: Maintainers matter

In the week of the 14 February we celebrated the "I Love Free Software Day 2026: Maintainers matter" ❤️. During our 2026 celebrations we highlighted the work done by Free Software maintainers while also reaching out to everybody contributing to Free Software.

We marked this special day with 22 events in 12 different European countries. What is more, hundreds of people and many organisations joined us in the Fediverse - with a special "Thank you!" note for Free Software projects, maintainers and contributors. To learn more about the 2026 celebration, you can read the full report.

Edition 2025: Hidden Projects

In the week of the 14th of February we celebrated the "I Love Free Software Day 2025: Hidden Projects" ❤️. Together with our local groups we wanted to raise awareness of all the hidden Free Software projects that power our daily infrastructure.

In 2025 we marked this special day with 19 events in 11 different European countries. What is more, hundreds of people and many organisations joined us in the Fediverse - with a special "Thank you!" note for Free Software projects and contributors. To learn more about the 2025 celebration, you can read the full report.

Edition 2024: Forging the future with Free Software

In the week of the 14th of February we celebrated the "I Love Free Software Day 2024: Forging the future with Free Software" ❤️. We focused on younger generations and how to introduce them to the Free Software community. As a result, several of our local groups celebrated this day with a meeting aimed at younger people. The rest just focused on the main "I Love Free Software Day" idea: to acknowledge the amazing Free Software community by thanking Free Software projects and sharing their love for Free Software.

While several FSFE local groups - in Italy, Denmark, the Netherlands, Spain, England, Germany, and Switzerland - got together and celebrated their love for software freedom in person, we were happy to see how other members of the Free Software community joined our call and organised meetings, like the one in Portugal. To learn more about the 2024 celebration, you can read the full report.

Edition 2023: Meet & Connect

In the week of the 14th of February we celebrated the "I Love Free Software Day 2023: Meet & Connect" ❤️. The FSFE organised several meetups and events all across Europe. The FSFE local groups in Spain, England, Portugal, Greece, Germany, and Switzerland took up the call of the FSFE to get together and celebrate their love for software freedom in person. Even Free Software contributors from outside the FSFE joined the call and organised meetings, like the ones in Austria and Barcelona. To learn more about the 2023 celebration, you can read the full report.

Edition 2022: Free Software Games

In the week of the 14th of February we celebrated the "I Love Free Software Day 2022: Free Software games" ❤️. We organised an event at the FSFE with four speakers, who took us into the world of Free Software games, and we also played the Free Software game Veloren. Edition 2021

In the week of the 14th of February we celebrated the "I Love Free Software Day 2021" ❤️. With our yearly #ilovefs, we asked you to thank your favourite developers and projects. Without offline events due to the pandemic, we tried two new things: share pictures to create your own personalised message, and a special Software Freedom Podcast episode about the history of the day.
