SFP#55: Policy and EU: Meet our Danish local group from Aarhus!

The Software Freedom Podcast for September is out! In this episode Bonnie Mehring meets with Carsten Agger, the FSFE’s Danish Coordinator. Together, they talk about the Free Software community in Denmark, digital sovereignty, and the growing interest in Free Software within the public sector.

Carsten shares his journey about how he became an active FSFE volunteer. Take a break and join us to learn about the founding of our Danish local group,about its current activities and their future plans. Bonnie and Carsten discuss Denmark’s increasing interest in digital independence, the role of Free Software in public infrastructure, and their upcoming plans with the FSFE’s "Public Money? Public Code!" initiative.

Listen to our 55th episode to learn more about the FSFE's local groups and its activities!

The work of the FSFE and its local groups is an important part of building a future where people can control the technology they use. You can support our work by donating today!
