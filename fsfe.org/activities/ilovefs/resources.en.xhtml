Resources

Our "I Love Free Software Day" quiz

Are you an expert on "I Love Free Software Day", or have you only just heard about it and aren’t quite sure what it is? To find out, you can take our dedicated quiz and see how much you know about the subject. Also, you can share it to help us raise awareness of this important day.

Sharepic Generator

A picture says more than a thousand words! You can use the sharepic generator to express your special gratitude and show your appreciation for Free Software by generating a picture that you can share on social media channels or add to your blog.

Order promotion material and merchandise

As informational material, we provide some "I Love Free Software" stickers, flyers, balloons, postcards, and posters to share at events, meetups, or within your community. You can order them at no cost on our informational material page.

In our merchandise shop, you can also get #ilovefs merchandise such as I love Free Software socks.

Other materials to use

We can provide a range of materials for your website to help illustrate "I Love Free Software Day":

  • Graphics: We provide you with different banners that you can use in yur website.
  • Photo gallery of people loving Free Software: In this gallery we collect some examples of people from all over the world expressing their enthusiasm for freedom in both software and society with photos.
