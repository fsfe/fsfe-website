Youth Hacking 4 Freedom

If you like programming, tinkering, and having fun with software, and you are up for a challenge, we have something exciting for you: "Youth Hacking 4 Freedom" (YH4F), the FSFE's hacking competition for young people from Europe! What's more, you can win up to €4,096 and a trip to Brussels for the awards ceremony!
