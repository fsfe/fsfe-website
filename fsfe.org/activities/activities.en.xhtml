Activities

Since its foundation in 2001, the FSFE has been working every single day to further software freedom in Europe and beyond. Beside the activities below, a large part of our work consists of continuous engagement and background work contributing at events and answering questions and enquiries around software freedom.

All Activities

Our vision is that everyone has the right to remove and install any software on any of their devices (Device Neutrality), that licensing and legal decisions are based on facts, rather than fear, uncertainty, and doubt (Legal Education), that all regulatory frameworks encourage the use and development of Free Software (Market Regulations), that all public funding for software is used for Free Software only (Public Money? Public Code!), that people enjoy to contribute to FSFE and feel empowered and effective (Volunteer Engagement), and that young people have the opportunity to tinker, experiment and code with Free Software as the default (Young Generation).
