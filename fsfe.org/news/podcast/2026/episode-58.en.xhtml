SFP#58: Policy and EU: The city of Dortmund, a digital sovereignty model!

In our newest episode of the Software Freedom Podcast, Bonnie Mehring talks with Christian Nähle, Managing Director of Do-FOSS, an initiative promoting software freedom in the city of Dortmund, Germany. They discuss how citizens can influence their local public bodies and help build a more independent digital future.

In this Friday's podcast episode, Chris and Bonnie talk about Do-FOSS and how a civil society initiative changed the city's digital strategy. Since 2014 to 2026, Do-FOSS has spent more than a decade building relationships with its city administration. Their efforts led the city to implement "Public Money? Public Code!" as a guiding principle. This resulted in a unanimous city council decision to adopt a strategy for strengthening the administration's technology sovereignty through Free Software.

In the 58th Software Freedom Podcast episode, Bonnie and Chris take a closer look at the work of Do-FOSS, the pitfalls they face and how to overcome them, and how its work can be a leading example for other municipalities. Together, Chris and Bonnie explore how digital sovereignty, based on Free Software, can become a reality for Germany's ninth-largest city.

Take a break and join us to learn more about how Dortmund is becoming a digitally sovereign city within the EU and how the FSFE is supporting Free Software across Europe!
