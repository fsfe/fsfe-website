Get Involved

Reach out to us to support Youth Hacking 4 Freedom

Do you want to get active and support Youth Hacking 4 Freedom? Do you want to help us inspire the next generation to build technology based on creativity and Free Software? Or do you just want to share the news about Youth Hacking 4 Freedom with your family and friends?

We would love to hear from you! Whether you are interested in sharing information about the programme, distributing materials, promoting YH4F within your community, or exploring other ways to get involved, feel free to reach out to us via yh4f@fsfe.org!

We are always happy to connect with organisations, communities, educators, and individuals who want to help spread the word and support the next generation of young hackers.

Order stickers

As informational material, we provide some "Youth Hacking 4 Freedom" stickers to share at events, meetups, schools, or within your community. You can order them at no cost on our informational material page.
