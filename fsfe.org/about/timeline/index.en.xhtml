FSFE Timeline - Working for Free Software since 2001

With the support of our supporters and our donors, the FSFE has been promoting and defending all aspects of software freedom in Europe since 2001. Take a look at our most important achievements in different areas with our timeline.

In the timeline we distinguish between the FSFE's activity frames:

Device Neutrality - everyone has the right to remove and install any software on any of their devices.

Legal Education - licensing and legal decisions are based on facts, rather than fear, uncertainty, and doubt.

Market Regulations - all regulatory frameworks encourage the use and development of Free Software.

Organisational Development - be an effective and efficient organisation for software freedom.

Public Money? Public Code! - all public funding for software is used for Free Software only.

Volunteer Engagement - people enjoy to contribute to FSFE and feel empowered and effective.

Young Generation - young people have the opportunity to tinker, experiment and code with Free Software as the default.
